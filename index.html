Install or update the ZIP preview in Chrome
  1. Download the ZIP and extract it into a folder you will keep. Do not load the ZIP directly or edit its manifest.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
  3. Select the extracted folder containing manifest.json. Open CriteriaCue from the toolbar to begin setup.
  4. To update, replace the files in that same folder with the new preview package, then click Reload on the extension. Keep the folder and avoid uninstalling if you want to retain its local roles.

The preview and the store version are separate installations. Their roles and sign-in do not migrate automatically. Avoid running both on the same profile. The ZIP is an installation package, not offline AI: an internet connection and email sign-in are required.

A few things to know

What does “currently free” mean?

The current V2 platform service is free for registered users, subject to service limits. There is no API-key setup or membership purchase step. This describes the V2 preview, not the earlier Chrome store release.

What happens to the information?

Roles, JDs and preferences are saved locally. For AI processing, the selected JD, final requirements and captured professional text are sent through the MINUS platform to Alibaba Cloud Bailian as needed. Our application database stores account and task metadata, not JD bodies, candidate text or analysis results. Candidate text and results are held in the current panel’s memory. Optional extension usage analytics remain off until you enable them; no candidate content, profile URLs or requirements are included. See the extension privacy policy for provider processing and retention details, and the website privacy notice for this site’s limited statistics.

Does it decide who to hire?

Detailed analysis includes an advisory 0–100 recommendation score, supported strengths, gaps and points to verify. The score is not a calibrated probability. CriteriaCue does not automatically rank, reject, hire or contact candidates. Missing information is not proof of a mismatch. Recruiters remain responsible for checking the evidence and making decisions.

Can I use the ZIP without an internet connection?

No. “ZIP” describes the installation method. Email sign-in and AI processing require an internet connection. The unpacked preview requires manual updates.

How can I give feedback?

Email support@minus.team. Share what you tried and what happened, without candidate details or credentials. Feedback does not subscribe you to marketing.
