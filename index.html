Install or update the ZIP preview in Chrome or Edge
  1. Download the ZIP and extract it into a folder you will keep. Do not load the ZIP directly or edit its manifest.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or edge://extensions in Edge, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
  3. Select the extracted folder containing manifest.json. Open CriteriaCue from the toolbar to begin setup.
  4. To update, replace the files in that same folder with the new preview package, then click Reload on the extension. Keep the folder and avoid uninstalling if you want to retain its local roles.

The preview and the store version are separate installations. Their roles and sign-in do not migrate automatically. Avoid running both on the same profile. The ZIP is an installation package, not offline AI: an internet connection and email sign-in are required.

A few things to know

What does “currently free” mean?

The current platform service is free for registered users, subject to usage limits. Email sign-in is required. No API key or membership purchase is needed. This describes the current offer; it is not a promise of unlimited use or permanent free access.

What happens to profile text and job descriptions?

Roles, JDs and preferences are saved locally in the extension. For AI features, the required JD, final requirements and captured professional text are sent through the MINUS backend, hosted on Railway in Singapore, to Alibaba Cloud Bailian’s currently configured Beijing-region endpoint. Email-code delivery uses Resend.

The backend keeps encrypted diagnostic copies of submitted tasks, including JDs, professional text and model output, for 7 days, accessible only to authorized administrators. Minimized, deidentified examples may still contain professional information and may remain in a restricted, encrypted internal regression library until removed by an operator. Account and basic task metadata have no fixed automatic deletion schedule. Operational backups and external providers have separate retention terms.

For provider details, retention and deletion requests, read the extension privacy policy or contact support@minus.team. Optional extension usage analytics require your active choice and exclude profile text and URLs, JDs, requirements and analysis results. This site’s limited statistics are described in the website privacy notice.

Does it decide who to hire?

Completed deep analysis includes an advisory 0–100 recommendation score, supported strengths, gaps and points to verify. The score is not a calibrated probability. CriteriaCue does not automatically rank, reject, hire or contact candidates. Missing information is not proof of a mismatch. You verify the evidence and make the decisions.

What can the extension read?

With your website permission, CriteriaCue reads the visible professional sections of the current LinkedIn profile as you scroll. It does not open other profiles, scroll or expand sections for you, read messages or contact details, or collect general browsing history. Close the panel to stop reading, or turn off Auto-review in Settings to pause automatic AI requests. Use only information you are authorized to process.

Can I use the ZIP without an internet connection?

No. “ZIP” describes the installation method. Email sign-in and AI processing require an internet connection. The v0.2.51 manual preview requires you to install future updates yourself; store installations update through their store.

How can I give feedback?

Email support@minus.team. Share what you tried and what happened, without candidate details or credentials. Feedback does not subscribe you to marketing.
